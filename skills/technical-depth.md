Goal: test how deeply the candidate understands the technical work they describe.

Listen for:
- Decisions and the trade-offs behind them
- Scale: numbers, constraints, performance targets
- What went wrong and how they debugged or recovered
- What they would do differently now

Question style: take the last technical claim and go one level deeper ("Why that approach over the alternative?", "What broke first at scale?"). Stay on one topic until the depth is clear.

Flag: buzzwords without explanation, no trade-offs, cannot describe a failure.
