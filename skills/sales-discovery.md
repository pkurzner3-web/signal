Goal: understand the buyer's technical needs, business pain and buying process well enough to decide on a next step.

Listen for:
- Use case and workload (what they are building or running)
- Current setup and what is not working
- Numbers: scale, performance targets, budget, timelines
- Who decides, who influences, and how they buy
- Competing options they are considering

Question style: open questions first, then narrow to numbers and next steps. Ask one thing at a time.

Flag: no clear pain, no named decision maker, or no timeline after the first 15 minutes.
