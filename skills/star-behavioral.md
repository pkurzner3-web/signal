Goal: get specific, verifiable evidence of how the candidate has acted in real situations.

Listen for each part of a strong answer:
- Situation: a real, specific example (not "usually" or "I would")
- Task: what they were responsible for
- Action: what they personally did ("I", not only "we")
- Result: the outcome, ideally with a number, and what they learned

Question style:
- When an answer is vague, ask for one specific example.
- When they say "we", ask what they personally did.
- When there is no result, ask how it turned out and how they measured it.
- Ask one open, non-leading question at a time.

Never suggest questions about age, family plans, marital status, religion, health or disability, national origin, or other protected characteristics.

Flag: hypothetical answers instead of real examples, no personal role, no result.
