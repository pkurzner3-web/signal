Goal: qualify the opportunity by covering each MEDDICC element.

Listen for:
- Metrics: the measurable outcome the buyer wants (cost, speed, revenue)
- Economic buyer: who controls the budget
- Decision criteria: how they will compare options
- Decision process: steps, people and timeline to a decision
- Paper process: legal, security and procurement steps
- Identify pain: the business problem and its cost
- Champion: who inside is pushing for this
- Competition: alternatives, including doing nothing

Question style: ask about the element least covered so far. One question at a time.

Flag: no economic buyer named, no metric, or no timeline after 15 minutes.
